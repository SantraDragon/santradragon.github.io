Probatarium: 3D Stickman Arena

A real-time 1v1 arena duel game for Android, made by SantraDragon Studios.

Two fighters, one arena, best of three rounds. Attacks, dodges and thrown weapons are simulated in lockstep so both players see exactly the same match.
