Privacy Policy

Probatarium: 3D Stickman Arena · SantraDragon Studios · last updated 13 September 2026

This policy explains what the game collects and why. It applies to the Android application com.santradragonstudios.probatarium.

What we collect

  • Nickname. The name you choose is shown to your opponent during a match.
  • Player account. Signing in creates an anonymous player id through Unity Gaming Services. Your cosmetics, currency balances and season progress are stored against that id.
  • IP address. Used by the multiplayer service to connect the two players while a match is running. It is not stored by us.
  • Match inputs. Button presses and movement are exchanged between the two players during a match and discarded when the match ends.
  • Purchases. If you buy in-game currency, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or billing details. We store the Google order identifier so the same purchase cannot be credited twice.

What we do not collect

No name, e-mail address or phone number. No location. No device identifiers or advertising id. The game contains no advertising and no analytics.

Stored on your device

Only your own settings - language, frame rate, touch sensitivity. Uninstalling the game removes them.

Services we use

  • Photon (Exit Games GmbH, Germany) - online match infrastructure.
  • Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies) - player accounts and saved progress.
  • Google Play - distribution and payments.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared for advertising, or handed to data brokers.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from them.
